At Brevo, we’re not just building a CRM. With our technology, we’re helping millions of organizations build lasting relationships with their customers. From emails and SMS to WhatsApp, Chat, and Marketing Automation, our tools are intuitive, powerful, and built to scale with every ambition. As Senior Sales Enablement Manager , you will own the enablement program for Brevo's Enterprise (ENT) go-to-market organization: ramping new AEs fast, embedding MEDDPICC as our sales methodology, and equipping managers to coach to one standard. You will be one of the first dedicated hires building this function for the ENT business unit, with real ownership over how it is designed, measured and scaled. Your impact at Brevo: New-Hire Onboarding & Ramp: design and run role-based 30/60/90 onboarding with certification gates, driving AE ramp through starter pipeline programs Sales Methodology, Skills & Coaching: drive MEDDPICC adoption as the ENT qualification standard, run deal reviews and coaching programs, and own the enablement partner relationship end-to-end Content, Playbooks & Product Enablement: build and maintain playbooks, battlecards, MAP templates and competitive intelligence, and govern content adoption Tooling, Data & Program Management: drive adoption of the enablement stack (Gong, Trumpet, ChiliPiper, CRM, Notion), own program governance, and report on enablement KPIs (ramp time, win rate, forecast accuracy) Strategic Planning & Leadership Enablement: shape the ENT enablement roadmap, equip leadership with coaching data and standards, and extend enablement coverage toward CSM and other teams Who you are: 5+ years in Sales/Revenue Enablement, Sales Training, or a sales teams, with hands-on knowledge of a structured sales methodology (MEDDPICC, Challenger, Value Selling, or similar) Track record building sales content (playbooks, battlecards, MAP templates,
