About the Role: We are seeking an experienced and strategic Director of EMEA Marketing to lead the marketing function across Europe. This role owns the EMEA marketing function end-to-end, partnering with global marketing leadership on shared standards while holding full accountability for regional strategy and results, reporting into our GM of EMEA . The Director will lead the EMEA marketing organization through a team of managers, each owning a discipline (demand gen, partner marketing, content), and will reconcile competing priorities across sales, product, corporate leadership, and global functional heads as the senior voice for EMEA.
