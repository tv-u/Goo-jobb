Job Description

Help us make a big green dent in the universe. We're on a mission to drive the global renewable energy revolution, and we need many more people to help us make our vision a reality. Octopus Energy Generation (OEGEN) is a leader in sourcing, investing in, and managing a wide range of utility-scale renewable energy assets. As we continue to expand our investment base, we are dedicated to building a diverse team that reflects the communities we serve. Currently, we manage a global portfolio of over £8 billion in renewable energy assets. About the role: As part of our mission to drive smarter energy decisions, we are building a production-grade, governed platform that empowers our teams to deploy AI-enabled tools, agents, and data products safely and at scale . Sitting within the Systems and Data / Transformation team, you will turn existing platform architectures, governance specs, and prototype patterns into production-grade infrastructure . You’ll work alongside our Tech and Transformation Lead, data engineers, application builders, and business stakeholders across Finance, Asset Management, and Fund Operations . This is not a general DevOps or blank-page architecture role . The primary focus is designing and implementing enterprise-grade identity, access, and governance controls across human users, systems, and non-human agents . Our Tech & Ecosystem: Databricks, Lakebase, Unity Catalog, Entra ID / Azure Identity, Okta, ZScaler, GitHub Actions, MCP patterns, and Terraform . What you'll do Platform Engineering & CI/CD Build and maintain Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC) using Terraform across development, test, and production environments . Strengthen CI/CD practices in GitHub Actions, establishing repeatable deployment patterns, environment promotions, automated rollbacks, and release controls . Support architecture decisions by converting designs into maintainable, well-documented engineering patterns . Identity, Access & Security Design and implement federated identity architecture across Okta, Entra ID, and ZScaler . Build identity, token exchange, and permission propagation models that work for both human users and non-human AI agents . Implement SCIM provisioning/deprovisioning and access models with single-revoke capability and full auditability . AI Gateway & Governance Harden existing AI gateway and agent orchestration layers (built on MCP patterns) into secure, production-ready capabilities . Implement API gateway controls: authentication, routing, rate limiting, token budgets, policy-as-code, and action logging . Build platform-, tool-, and agent-level kill switches, alongside dry-run/safe-testing modes for agent workflows . Observability & FinOps Implement AI system observability, including prompt logging, output monitoring, quality scoring, and drift detection . Establish operational monitoring for gateway usage, latency, error rates, and model routing logic . Enforce token budgets, cost alerts, and usage/cost attribution by team and use case . Enablement & Integration Deploy vector database and retrieval infrastructure (e.g., Databricks AI Search) to support Document AI and RAG use cases . Align platform controls with Databricks and Unity Catalog governance patterns . Provide clear documentation, reusable patterns, and self-service onboarding guidance so delivery squads can deploy tools without bespoke manual intervention . What you'll need Federated Identity Mastery: Proven experience designing and implementing enterprise-grade federated identity, token exchange, permission propagation, SCIM provisioning, and access lifecycle management . Platform Foundations: Strong hands-on experience with IaC (Terraform) and production CI/CD pipelines (environment promotion, rollback, release controls) . API Gateways: Experience hardening API gateway patterns (authentication, routing, rate limiting, access policy enforcement) . Cloud & Data Environments: Technical comfort working across Azure, AWS, or Databricks platform ecosystems . Pragmatic Mindset: Highly autonomous engineer comfortable in a lean team; skilled at translating architectural specs into practical code and communicating trade-offs clearly in plain English And to really stand out from the crowd Experience with AI gateways, LLM proxies, or model-routing patterns . Exposure to AI observability tools, vector databases (Databricks AI Search), RAG pipelines, or non-deterministic system monitoring . Hands-on experience implementing policy-as-code, action logging, or AI kill switches . Direct experience with Okta, ZScaler, Entra ID, Unity Catalog, or M365 migration environments . Background in regulated or high-governance industries (Financial Services, Energy, Infrastructure Investment, Fintech, or Enterprise SaaS) .
