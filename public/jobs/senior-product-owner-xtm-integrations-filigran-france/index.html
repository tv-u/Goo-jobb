You’ll work closely with Product Managers, Engineering, and Customer teams to prioritize Integrations, prototype them and bring them to life — from idea to delivery. 💼 Your Responsibilities Define and manage the product roadmap for Integrations across the product range, notably OpenCTI and OpenAEV.
