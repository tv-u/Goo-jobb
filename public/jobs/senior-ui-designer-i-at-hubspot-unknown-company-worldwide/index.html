You’ll also explore AI-assisted design and creative tools to accelerate the work while keeping strong design judgment, user needs, accessibility, and brand principles at the center. &#xA;&#xA; What You’ll Do &#xA;&#xA; &#xA; Lead visual design direction for major Marketing projects and digital experiences from early concepts through implementation and launch. &#xA; Translate user needs, business goals, and brand strategy into intuitive, polished, accessible interface experiences. &#xA;
