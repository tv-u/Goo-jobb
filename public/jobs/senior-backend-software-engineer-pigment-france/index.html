Who You Are: We value strong software engineering skills and a problem-solving mindset. You thrive on turning complex requirements into elegant, maintainable solutions and are eager to deliver value to end users. In any case, you have: - Experience working as a software engineer on production systems. - Proven ability to design object-oriented software with languages such as C#, Java, C++, Golang, Rust, Python, or Ruby. - A collaborative mindset and strong communication skills. - A desire to deeply understand how the product works and the value it brings to users. - A good dose of humility and the willingness to grow (no matter your seniority!). - A fluent level in English (French is not required). Current Projects You could Work On : Computation Engine : Scaling and optimizing our formula engine to support increasingly complex use cases and larger datasets.
