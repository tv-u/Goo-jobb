Job Description

Your mission The Bikeleasing Group is one of the leading companies in the field of company bike leasing. Through Probonio, we also offer modern employee benefits and help shape future-oriented working environments with our brands. We are continuing to grow.As Senior Digital Creative & AI Champion (gn), you will raise the creative and conceptual standard of our digital communication. You will develop concepts for digital content and smaller projects, take ownership of AI in creative production and establish AI-powered video production as a new capability within the team. You will work closely with the Head of Content & Creation, the Creative and Content team, Marketing, digital channel owners and internal stakeholders. This role is primarily remote. If you combine creative quality with a solution-oriented mindset, ownership and a strong interest in emerging technologies, we look forward to hearing from you. You develop creative and conceptual approaches for digital content and campaigns You translate your concepts into concrete designs and create digital assets hands-on yourself You give smaller projects a clear conceptual foundation and support them from the initial idea through to the visual execution You establish AI-powered video production as an independent capability within the team and continue to develop it You contribute your perspective to creative processes, standards and workflows Your profile You have strong creative and conceptual skills for digital formats You have experience using AI in creative production and can assess practical use cases You have worked with a range of digital channels and content formats You can also execute ideas and content visually yourself and ideally have an art direction background You work independently, take a solution-oriented approach and move topics forward in a pragmatic way You have very good German language skills at C1 level or above Why us? You will join a fast-growing company where you can make an impact and actively shape new ideas You benefit from maximum flexibility, supported by our modern flexitime model and workation policy We value open communication, honest feedback and collaboration on equal terms Through Probonio, you receive a monthly €50 voucher of your choice plus €60 each year on your birthday In addition to strong insurance conditions for your company pension plan, you benefit from capital-forming benefits You can lease up to two bicycles or pedelecs through us as your employer We see diversity as an enrichment for our team.
