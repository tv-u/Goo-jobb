Job Description

Are you looking for a new challenge? Fancy helping us shape the future of motor insurance? Prima could be the place for you. And now we’re expanding to help millions more drivers in the UK and Spain. To help fuel that growth, we need a Senior Data Analyst to join our Claims UK team.
