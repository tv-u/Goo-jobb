Job Description

About us Founded in 2019 and part of Y Combinator's 2020 cohort, Photoroom is the leading visual solution for e-commerce. We've raised Series B funding and reached 300+ million users worldwide, processing over 5 billion images annually and serving both small businesses and major enterprises like Amazon, DoorDash, and Decathlon through our mobile app, web platform, and API. We're a remote-friendly team of 100+ passionate builders giving e-commerce businesses superpowers to create visuals that help them grow, making the hardest parts of selling online disappear. We focus on craft, innovation, and collaboration, creating exceptional impact for e-commerce businesses worldwide. Role Summary We're looking for a Senior User Acquisition Manager to own and scale Meta — Photoroom's largest paid channel — as we shift toward acquiring SMBs at scale. You'll drive spend, ROAS, and SMB growth on Meta and act as the connective tissue between paid acquisition and our in-house creative team, turning performance signals into the creative hypotheses that win. You'll work hand-in-hand with our Creative team, our Data/measurement function, and Product. Compensation: 60,000€ to 70,000€ + Stock-Options/BSPCE Location: Work from Paris, with a hybrid model in our HQ office About the Role Own and scale Meta (7-figure monthly budget), consistently hitting ROAS and LTV:CAC targets while accelerating the shift of spend toward SMB/business audiences. Drive the creative feedback loop end-to-end: mine Meta performance for what wins and what loses, generate creative hypotheses, and give the in-house creative team structured, data-backed direction so the pipeline of winning ads never runs dry. Build and run a high-velocity testing engine across audiences, creatives, campaign structures, and micro-personas — shipping fast to learn and compounding insights into the SMB pivot. Connect channel performance to unit economics and incrementality, partnering on attribution and measurement so spend decisions reflect true incremental value rather than last-click. Work AI-native: build your own tooling and automations to make UA faster and more autonomous (we're an AI company — internal tools, automation, and agentic workflows are encouraged and celebrated here). About You 4+ years in performance marketing with deep, hands-on Meta expertise, managing six-figure monthly budgets against LTV:CAC targets. A proven track record of partnering closely with creative teams. You don't just consume creative, you generate hypotheses from data, brief, and close the loop. Strong analytical depth: comfortable with attribution, incrementality, and unit economics, and able to tie channel decisions to business outcomes. High agency and ownership: you take ambiguous, undefined problems and drive them through to shipped outcomes without waiting to be told. A candid, collaborative, team-first operator who makes the people around you better. You communicate openly, give and take feedback well, and strengthen the whole team. An AI-native way of working — you build tools and automate workflows (e.g. with Claude Code or similar) rather than waiting on eng/data. [Nice-to-have] Experience marketing to SMBs or in PLG/SaaS (Canva, Shopify, Wix, Vista-type environments). If you think you have what it takes but don't meet every single point above, please still apply. We'd love to chat and see if you could be a great fit. Benefits Regular team gatherings, including in-person onboarding in Paris (1-2 weeks), yearly company offsite and team retreat, quarterly in-person meetings (monthly during probation period), and social events like winter party and hackathons. 30 days annual leave plus local public holidays Competitive equity package with stock options/BSPCE, giving you ownership in our growing company €1,000 annual learning and development budget for training, courses, books, and skill development Private health insurance Access to personalized mental health support , including 1:1 sessions with therapists or coaches, self-care tools, and well-being resources via MokaCare Sports and cultural activities reimbursement Relocation support (up to 10k) available for those choosing to move to France, including apartment finding and visa assistance. Note: Visa sponsorship and relocation support are only available for positions based in France. We cannot sponsor visas for remote positions outside of France.
