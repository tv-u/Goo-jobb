Please send your CV in English About Diabolocom Diabolocom enables organizations worldwide to build brand loyalty and drive sales growth through its cloud-based contact center platform. Our solution leverages AI-driven technologies to optimize customer experience across all communication channels. We provide seamless management of inbound and outbound interactions, premium voice quality, real-time analytics, and global scalability. Our platform integrates with leading CRMs and offers a wide range of APIs and services in multiple languages. With offices across Europe, the USA, Brazil, and the UAE, and more than 350 clients operating in over 60 countries, we are continuously growing and strengthening our engineering teams. We are looking for a SEO manager (German market) to join our team. You will be part of our global marketing department, working closely with the SEO team and the local DACH region teams. Your SEO responsibilities include: ● Optimizing existing content and creating new content tailored to the German market's SEO requirements, including landing pages, blog articles, case studies, etc. ● Tracking and managing SEO performance for the German market using tools like Google Search Console, SEMrush, Screaming Frog, Ahrefs and Google Analytics. ● Monitoring search trends and identifying SEO opportunities specific to Germany. ● Conducting competitive analysis for the German market. ● Contributing to long-term SEO strategy development for the DACH region (Germany, Austria, Switzerland). ● Working on a lead-generation strategy based on SEO for the German market. ● Managing website content migration. ● Defining and implementing URL redirection plans. ● Conducting page testing and quality assurance (QA) before launch. Digital Marketing responsibilities: ● Collaborating on digital marketing campaigns targeting the DACH market, including local digital media platforms and email marketing. ● Assisting in managing social media content (LinkedIn posts, press releases, etc.) in German. What We’re Looking For ● Expertise in SEO for the German market: solid understanding of site structures, Google SEO guidelines, and HTML tagging for SEO. ● Proficiency with SEO tools such as SEMRush, Screaming Frog, Google Search Console, and Google Analytics. ● Strong writing and optimization skills for content in German. ● Experience with a CMS, WordPress and Webflow is a plus. ● Experience with email marketing tools, Hubspot or Lemlist is a plus. ● Previous experience in B2B SaaS General Skills: ● Consultative mindset with the ability to understand business needs, challenge stakeholders constructively, and provide value-driven recommendations. ● Strong project management skills, with the ability to manage multiple tasks independently. ● Native in German with good command of English (C1). French is a plus. ● Strong interest in the tech industry, especially in AI and SaaS (Software as a Service). ● Excellent writing and communication skills. ● Experience in SEA for the German market is a plus.
