Headquarters: Switzerland URL: http://www.kazaarfragrances.com Brand & Creative Designer (Freelance, 100% Remote) Kazaar Fragrances · European Market Leader in Perfume Dupes · Full-Time · CET Hours About Us We're Kazaar Fragrances ( kazaarfragrances.com ) the European market leader in high-quality fragrance dupes, and profitable for over 6 years. Our core markets are Germany, Austria, and Switzerland. Beyond our main brand, our portfolio includes additional projects in health/wellness and a second fragrance-focused brand (more on these once we're talking). We're 100% remote across time zones, with core collaboration happening in Central European Time (CET). Day-to-day, things are structured and predictable. Around major sales periods (Black Friday, big launches), it gets fast-paced and intense. About the Role You'll join our 2-person design team (making us 3), reporting directly to our Design Lead / Co-Founder and Marketing Lead. You'll work as an all-round creative across our brand portfolio, with focus on our main brand and support on others as needed. Type: Full-time freelance / independent contractor Hours: 45 hrs/week, Monday–Friday Location: 100% remote, based in Europe Start: ASAP or September, 2026 Reports to: Design Lead & Marketing Lead What You'll Work On Roughly in order of time spent: Ad creative & motion design for our paid social/ad accounts. Static and animated graphics Day-to-day campaign design newsletters, email design, sales creatives Web & UI/UX optimization Packaging design Social media content AI-assisted image generation, editing, and optimization (Claude, Gemini, etc.) Some projects come with tight briefs; others give you real creative freedom. Expect a genuine mix. What We're Looking For Must-Have Expert-level Figma skills. our primary design tool. All candidates complete a Figma design test as part of our process. Hands-on experience using AI tools (Claude, Gemini, etc.) for image generation and editing A genuine eye for design Fluent to native-level English. This is our working language, non-negotiable Based in Europe, working hours overlapping CET, with a valid right to work as a freelancer/self-employed professional in your country of residence Comfortable working independently and asynchronously; we communicate via Slack Adobe Suite experience (Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects) Very Good to Have (this will significantly boost your chances) Motion design skills. animated graphics, explainer/product animations, video ad elements 3D / Blender skills creating 3D Stills and Videos German, most of our creative work is produced in German, so it's a real plus, but AI-assisted translation covers this if you don't speak German A Mac/macOS setup We care more about your portfolio and what you can do than your years of experience or where you studied. Strong junior talent with an excellent portfolio is welcome to apply. What We Offer Real ownership and creative freedom, clear goals, no micromanagement Work across multiple brands, so it never gets boring Direct line to the founders, fast decisions, short feedback loops Growth potential as the team scales, including leadership opportunities based on performance Fully remote, flexible scheduling as long as deadlines are met €1,000–3,500/month (freelance basis), depending fully on your performance, portfolio and experience. negotiable, and strongly performance-based.
