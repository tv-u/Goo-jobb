Headquarters: Remote URL: http://onthegosystems.com At OnTheGoSystems , we’ve been building successful translation and localization software with a fully remote, international team since 2007. Our products are used on more than 1.5 million active WordPress sites, helping businesses around the world build and manage multilingual websites and content. We’re looking for a technical, hands-on Technical Paid Advertising Specialist who knows how to connect AI, automation, and data to real advertising workflows. What you'll do This is a role where you’ll have the freedom to build, test, and improve, not just manage campaigns designed by someone else. You’ll own paid advertising hands-on while having the space to explore how AI, automation, and better use of data can improve the way campaigns are managed. You’ll be able to experiment with new tools and approaches, build your own solutions, and directly influence how we develop a more technical and AI-driven approach to paid advertising. We’d especially like to meet someone who: Has personally set up, run, analyzed, and optimized paid campaigns. Has hands-on experience with platforms such as Google Ads, Meta Ads, Microsoft Ads, or LinkedIn Ads. Has used technical tools to automate, connect, analyze, or improve advertising workflows. This could include Google Ads Scripts, advertising APIs, Python, Zapier, Make, GA4, BigQuery, Looker Studio, and AI agents. Is comfortable managing several campaigns or channels at the same time. Can show real examples of using AI or automation to solve advertising problems or reduce manual work. Thinks analytically and enjoys investigating why performance changes, not just reporting that it changed. Understands that automation needs validation and can explain how they check that automated workflows are working correctly. Can work independently, prioritize what needs attention, and move things forward without detailed instructions. Enjoys experimenting with new technology and turning useful ideas into working solutions. What we offer 100% remote position, Body and Mind Movement program to fill you up with energy, A computer budget to make your workplace better, A Kindle device with access to our company Amazon account, We respect national holidays in each country and want you to have a rest these days, Great opportunity to progress and advance, Collaborating with team members across the globe. If you enjoy paid advertising as much as you enjoy building smarter ways to run it, we'd love to hear from you. Tell us about the campaigns you've owned and the AI or automation you've built into your work. To apply: https://weworkremotely.com/remote-jobs/onthegosystems-paid-ads-specialist
