Job Description

IDnow is a leader in digital identity and fraud prevention in Europe with a mission to transform trust into the most powerful asset in the digital world, empowering enterprises with AI-driven, SaaS-based identity solutions that deliver scalable security, adaptive compliance, and real-time fraud prevention . Through its broad portfolio of digital identity and fraud prevention solutions, IDnow establishes, maintains and enriches trust throughout the customer journey, ensuring businesses can confidently and securely operate while leveraging digital identity to drive growth, security and scalability. The company has offices in Germany, United Kingdom, Romania and France , and is backed by renowned institutional investors, including Corsair Capital and Seventure Partners. Its portfolio of international clients spans a wide range of end markets including financial services, telecommunications, travel & mobility, gaming, and other industries. This position is based in Rennes, France. We are looking for a hands-on QA Automation Engineer who can strengthen our product quality across Web, API, and Mobile platforms. In this role, you will build and maintain automated tests, validate end-to-end workflows, and support the stability of customer-facing integrations. You will collaborate with developers, embedded QA engineers, and platform teams to ensure releases meet customer expectations. Key Responsibilities Automation Develop and maintain automated test suites for Web, Mobile (Appium), and Backend API flows. Implement integration tests across microservices using mocks, stubs, and reference backservers. Contribute to end-to-end automation of real customer workflows. Ensure repeatability, scalability, and stability of automated tests. CI/CD & Tooling Integrate automated tests into CI/CD pipelines. Maintain test reporting and monitoring dashboards. Contribute to test data setup and staging environment readiness. Collaboration Work closely with developers, embedded QAs, Product Owners, and DevOps. Engage early in feature development to identify risks and testability concerns. Support continuous improvement of automation and testing practices. Preferred Experience 2+ years of experience as a QA Engineer with similar responsibilities. Strong experience with Python . Experience with Selenium / Playwright for web automation. Hands-on experience with Appium for mobile automation. Strong REST API testing using Python requests. Knowledge of microservices and integration testing. CI/CD exposure and understanding of pipeline stages. Strong debugging mindset (logs, tracing, network calls). Self-driven, proactive, and hands-on. Strong debugging and analytical mindset. Capable of owning automation tasks end-to-end. Comfortable across backend, web, and mobile automation. Fluent English and working proficiency in French is required. Nice to Have: Contract testing (JSON Schema, Pact). Experience testing mobile SDK flows. Familiarity with test containers/emulators. Experience in identity verification or security domains.
