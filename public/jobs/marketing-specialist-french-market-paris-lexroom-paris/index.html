Job Description

At Query Juriste, we're building the legal AI that reasons like a lawyer — reliable, sourced, and designed for legal professionals. In just a few months, what started as an idea has become a Legal AI platform used daily by more than 250 law firms and companies in France, including references such as Bredin Prat, Gibson Dunn, Jeantet, Veil Jourde, and De Pardieu Brocas Maffei. Our mission is clear: make legal research faster, more reliable, and more rigorous, giving lawyers and legal professionals the time to focus on what matters most — reasoning and advising. We're accelerating our growth, backed by top-tier investors and a demanding team driven by rigor and impact. Our data and our clients' data stay hosted in Europe, encrypted and isolated by firm — trust is at the core of our product and our culture. Today, Query Juriste is establishing itself as a partner of reference, and we're building a strong team to become the market leader in legal AI in France. Join one of Query Juriste's first marketing hires, and build the brand from the ground up as we support the company through its next chapter. Why now? Set the Standard: Be part of the team defining the benchmark for legal AI in Europe. Work with the Best: Collaborate with demanding colleagues in a culture that values competence, curiosity, and ambition. Deliver Real Impact: Your work will directly influence how lawyers and legal professionals operate — faster, clearer, and more efficiently. At Query Juriste, we leave the buzzwords behind. Here, you'll build something that lasts: a trusted platform, a strong reputation, a new standard for the legal world. Your Mission as Marketing Specialist We're looking for a Marketing Specialist, ideally based in Paris, to lead the planning, coordination, and execution of Query Juriste's events, advertising campaigns, and social media community building. You will own initiatives end-to-end, from initial brief to post-activation analysis, ensuring operational excellence, brand consistency, and measurable impact across internal and external audiences. This role is for someone highly organized, proactive, and detail-oriented, who enjoys building a brand from the ground-up and thrives in the fast-paced, hands-on environment of a growing startup. Key Responsibilities Own events end-to-end, from scouting legal events across the region, initial brief and planning to execution and post-event debrief. Plan and coordinate external activations including trade fairs, conferences, webinars, and partner events. Build and grow Query Juriste's social media community through consistent, engaging content and a qualified database. Collaborate with HQ on marketing campaigns, adapting messaging, claims, and landing pages by campaign and geography. Analyze campaign and event performance across channels, regions, and creatives, identifying what works and what doesn't. Define clear objectives, timelines, and KPIs in collaboration with leadership, marketing, and partnerships. Coordinate with communications teams on event promotion, newsletters, and social content before and after activations. Track results and collect feedback to continuously improve events and campaigns. What You'll Bring 5-7 years of experience in marketing, ideally in a SaaS or fast-growing startup environment (if you have worked in Legal or have studied Law is definitely a plus but not a specific requirement). Strong creative and organizational skills, with meticulous attention to detail. A proactive, can-do attitude and the ability to handle multiple projects simultaneously. Passion for innovation, technology, and dynamic environments. Excellent written and spoken French and English. Familiarity with event and webinar management tools or visual content creation is a plus.
