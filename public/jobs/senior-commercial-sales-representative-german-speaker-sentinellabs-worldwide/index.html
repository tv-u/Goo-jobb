Our Purpose At SentinelOne, we are driven by a clear purpose: to give the advantage to those who secure our future. When you join SentinelOne, your work helps protect global enterprises, critical infrastructure, and the technologies shaping tomorrow. If you are motivated by meaningful challenges and want your impact to be real, measurable, and global, you will find purpose here. About Us SentinelOne is a company at the intersection of AI and security, pioneering a new operating model for cybersecurity. Our AI-native platform unifies protection across endpoint, cloud, identity, data, and AI systems to deliver autonomous detection and response with clarity and speed. By combining real-time analytics, intelligent automation, and a unified data foundation, we reduce noise, simplify complexity, and empower security teams to focus on what truly matters. Our teams are builders, problem-solvers, and innovators committed to shaping the future of security. If you are excited to solve hard problems alongside talented, mission-driven people, we invite you to help us build a safer future for humanity. What Are We Looking For? We’re looking for people who are relentlessly curious and committed to continuous learning. AI is reshaping every function across our business, and we enable every team member, regardless of role or level, to build fluency in AI tools and concepts. Those who thrive here actively seek out new solutions, experiment thoughtfully, and apply what they learn to drive better, faster, smarter outcomes. As a Senior Commercial Sales Representative you will accelerate SentinelOne's growth across accounts in Germany, taking ownership of expanding our commercial footprint by identifying opportunities, driving new business, and consistently surpassing ambitious sales goals. You will own the entire sales cycle — from prospecting to closing — building meaningful relationships with organizations and collaborating with our channel ecosystem to maximize success across your territory. If you are energized by competition, inspired by collaboration, and motivated to make an impact at a fast-moving cybersecurity leader, this is your opportunity to shine. What Will You Do? Primary responsibilities include: Own the full sales cycle from prospecting through to close, targeting accounts across Germany, qualifying opportunities, negotiating with key decision-makers, and delivering results through disciplined forecasting, pipeline management, and proactive account planning. Build and manage a high-performing pipeline by leveraging multiple lead sources, outbound strategies, and channel partnerships, while collaborating with field sales teams and cross-functional stakeholders to amplify reach and accelerate deal velocity. Act as a trusted advisor to customers by developing deep knowledge of SentinelOne's solutions and the broader cybersecurity landscape, delivering tailored, value-driven conversations that address each client's specific needs and challenges. Stay ahead of industry trends and competitive dynamics to continuously refine your consultative sales approach, and provide timely and accurate forecasts, insights, and strategic feedback to sales leadership. What Skills and Knowledge Will You Bring? Ideal candidates will have: Fluency in both English and German, with excellent written and verbal communication skills and exceptional presentation, storytelling, and objection-handling skills that build trust and inspire customers. At least 2 years of direct sales experience at a software vendor, with a proven record of closing net new logos, driving territory growth, and consistently exceeding targets in the commercial segment. Experience selling into commercial organizations, with a background in working with or through channel partners to maximize collaboration and reach. The ability to manage complex sales cycles from initial qualification through to executive-level negotiations, with comfort operating in a fast-paced, high-growth environment. Prior cybersecurity experience is a strong advantage, combined with a results-driven, competitive, and self-motivated mindset and a Bachelor's degree or equivalent professional experience. Why SentinelOne? AI is redefining how the world operates and rewriting the rules of security in real time, and SentinelOne was built for this moment.
