You’ll write production code, make and shape technical decisions, and support a small group of engineers.&lt;br&gt;This isn’t a role where you step away from engineering to manage from the side lines. You’ll be involved in the day-to-day work: thinking through architecture, reviewing code, tackling difficult problems and helping the team find sensible ways forward when things get complicated.&lt;br&gt;You’ll line manage 3–4 engineers, with regular 1:1s and responsibility for supporting their development. A big part of the role is helping other engineers get better at what they do — not by having all the answers, but by giving them the support and technical direction they need.&lt;/p&gt; &lt;p&gt;&lt;br&gt;You’ll work closely with delivery managers, designers, product managers, principal engineers and clients. Sometimes that means working through a difficult technical problem; sometimes it means challenging a requirement, explaining a trade-off or finding a simpler way to achieve the same thing.&lt;/p&gt; &lt;p&gt;&lt;br&gt;Most of our web work is built around TypeScript, React and Next.js, alongside APIs, cloud services, headless platforms and other backend technologies.&lt;/p&gt; &lt;p&gt; &lt;/p&gt; &lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;What You’ll Do&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt; &lt;ul&gt; &lt;li&gt;Write production code across the stack and stay involved in the day-to-day engineering work.&lt;/li&gt; &lt;li&gt;Take the lead on technical decisions within the team, bringing in principal engineers and others when decisions have wider implications.&lt;/li&gt; &lt;li&gt;Help turn large or difficult problems into work the team can understand and deliver.&lt;/li&gt; &lt;li&gt;Line manage 3–4 engineers through regular 1:1s, feedback, coaching and career conversations.&lt;/li&gt; &lt;li&gt;Review code, talk through designs and help engineers make good technical decisions rather than simply making those decisions for them.&lt;/li&gt; &lt;li&gt;Work with delivery, design, product and client teams to understand what we’re trying to achieve and find an appropriate technical approach.&lt;/li&gt; &lt;li&gt;Keep an eye on the things that make software good to work with and reliable in production: testing, CI/CD, observability, security, accessibility and performance.&lt;/li&gt; &lt;li&gt;Help design and evolve web platforms that integrate with APIs, headless systems and third-party services.&lt;/li&gt; &lt;li&gt;Contribute to the wider engineering team at Code, sharing what works, challenging what doesn’t and helping us improve how we build software.&lt;/li&gt; &lt;/ul&gt; &lt;p&gt; &lt;/p&gt; &lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;What We’re Looking For&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt; &lt;ul&gt; &lt;li&gt;You’ve probably already worked as a senior or lead engineer in a team building and shipping software.&lt;/li&gt; &lt;li&gt;You’re comfortable taking responsibility for technical decisions, but you also know when a decision needs wider discussion.&lt;/li&gt; &lt;li&gt;You’ll need strong hands-on TypeScript experience and good experience with modern web development using React, Next.js or similar frameworks.&lt;/li&gt; &lt;li&gt;You should also be comfortable working beyond the frontend. That might mean Node.js, C#/.NET or another server-side technology. We care more about your ability to understand APIs, backend services and how the whole system fits together than whether your experience exactly matches our stack.&lt;/li&gt; &lt;li&gt;We’d also expect you to be comfortable with many of the things that come with building modern web platforms: APIs, server-side rendering, automated testing, CI/CD, cloud services, web performance and integrating with third-party or headless systems.&lt;/li&gt; &lt;li&gt;You’ll need to be able to explain technical ideas clearly. Some conversations will be with engineers; others will be with designers, delivery people, clients or stakeholders who don’t particularly care how the codeworks. Being able to adjust how you communicate matters.&lt;/li&gt; &lt;li&gt;Experience line managing engineers is useful, but it isn’t essential if you’ve already spent time mentoring, coaching and supporting other developers and want to take on that responsibility formally.&lt;/li&gt; &lt;li&gt;We don’t expect you to know everything. Experience with Azure, AWS or GCP is useful, as is experience across both TypeScript and C#/.NET, but neither is a requirement.
